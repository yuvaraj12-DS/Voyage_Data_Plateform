# Business Requirements

## Business Context

VoyageData is a fictional European travel company operating
hotels and travel experiences across multiple destinations.

The company runs digital marketing campaigns across multiple
channels and receives bookings through its booking systems.

Currently, marketing and booking information exists in separate
systems, making it difficult to measure campaign performance
and understand the relationship between advertising spend and
booking revenue.

## Business Objective

Build a unified data platform that consolidates marketing,
booking, and website analytics data into a trusted analytical
layer.

## Primary Business Questions

1. Which campaigns generate the most revenue?
2. Which campaigns have the highest ROAS?
3. Which marketing channels generate the most bookings?
4. What is the conversion rate from click to booking?
5. Which destinations generate the most booking revenue?
6. Which campaigns have high spend but poor performance?
7. How does cancellation rate vary across marketing segments?
8. How much marketing spend is associated with each booking?

## Primary KPIs

- Marketing Spend
- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- CPC
- Conversions
- Conversion Rate
- Booking Revenue
- ROAS
- Booking Count
- Cancellation Rate
